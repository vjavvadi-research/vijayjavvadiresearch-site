Research Vision

Research Vision

Predict, prevent, attribute — the next decade of software quality

The long-term goal is a quality-engineering platform that doesn't just predict where defects will appear, but actively prevents them at code-review time and attributes them at runtime — with every model decision governed, auditable, and reproducible.

Three commitments

01

Empirical honesty over headline numbers

Every number we report is reproducible from on-disk artefacts. Bugs are disclosed, not buried — the May 2026 file_age_days correction is a worked example of this commitment, not an exception to it.

02

Calibration before raw accuracy

The deployed model is the one that ranks honestly, not the one with the highest AUC on a test set. Production selection criteria explicitly privilege calibration, top-k stability, and threshold sensitivity.

03

Governance is not an afterthought

Payload controls, prompt versioning, privacy hashing, and audit trails are part of the architecture, not a compliance bolt-on. Every model call produces a verifiable receipt.
