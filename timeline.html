Research Timeline

Research Timeline

From first experiments to publication, production, and professional service

A chronological view of the program's milestones — research, open-source releases, publication, and professional service — through October 2026.

2025 · Q3

Repository Analytics Engine v1

Initial extraction across five OSS systems; baseline 6-classifier benchmark; first FastAPI prototype.

2025 · Q4

First eight-paper draft series

Paper 1–Paper 8 outline. Identified that Paper 2 (dataset) and Paper 3 (classifier comparison) were thinly sliced and needed consolidation.

2026 · Q1

Self-healing locator framework

2,400 mutation events generated across seven refactor classes; tree-ensemble ranking models trained and calibrated. Paper D drafted.

2026 · Q1

RAITG & the test-generation service

312 requirements processed across banking, healthcare, e-commerce. 94.1% requirement coverage, 96.3% first-pass verify. Paper E drafted.

2026 · Q2

file_age_days bug discovered & fixed

Sign bug from git log's default ordering contaminated ~40% of rows. Replaced with explicit min/max accumulators. All datasets and models rebuilt; commit_frequency emerges as a real secondary signal.

2026 · May

Eight papers consolidated to six

Paper 1–Paper 6 of the defect series merged into Paper A (empirical study), Paper B (cross-repo), and Paper C (risk-based testing). Total page count drops from ~80–100 to 20 with no loss of empirical content.

2026 · May

Production model gb-paper1-v4-fixed_age

Gradient Boosting + SMOTE deployed in the FastAPI risk-prediction service. GitHub Actions integration with advisory and gating rollouts.

2026 · May

npm tooling published

The @vijaypjavvadi scope ships bdd2pw, sel2pw, and pw-emit, each archived on Zenodo with a DOI.

2026 · Jun

Core papers enter peer review

Paper B → Wiley JSEP; Paper D → Wiley STVR; Paper G1 → Taylor & Francis Information Security Journal. SSRN preprint of the bdd2pw article posted (9 Jun). Google Scholar profile goes live alongside the ORCID record with Crossref auto-push. TestForge AI launches on Product Hunt (9 Jun) and TestForge PaperQC enters free beta.

2026 · Jul

IEEE Global Career Fair 2026 — website team

Selected for the volunteer team of IEEE's first global, all-regions career fair; kick-off 15 Jul. Homepage redesign proposal and mockups presented to the program lead. The bdd2pw software article is submitted to Elsevier SoftwareX (7 Jul). The testforge meta-CLI is published to npm.

2026 · Aug

IEEE Senior Member · first journal publication

Elevated to IEEE Senior Member (8 Aug). The bdd2pw article is accepted and published online in Elsevier SoftwareX (vol. 35, October 2026 issue; DOI 10.1016/j.softx.2026.102933) — the program's first peer-reviewed journal publication. Admitted as a Fellow of the Institute of Analytics. Session Judge at the 2nd NextGen Hackathon 2026 (ACM Fremont Chapter, 15–16 Aug). Paper C → Springer Discover Computing; Paper G2 → Elsevier Computers & Security. Twelve IEEE conference reviews completed across ICEC, TEMSMET, IDSTA, and NIGERCON (Jun–Aug).

2026 · Sep

careerfair.ieee.org goes live · Scopus-indexed conference paper

The redesigned IEEE Global Career Fair homepage, Events hub, About page, and site navigation are published on IEEE's production site; publicly credited as Website Team Co-Lead. The ICOSAAS 2026 flaky-tests paper is indexed in Scopus. Paper E → Wiley STVR; Paper F (reframed on 1,323 real defects) → PeerJ Computer Science. pw-self-heal, synthdata, and agentic-qa published to npm; collaboration with Boni García (UC3M) and Ricca & Leotta (Genoa) on browser-automation adoption begins.

2026 · Oct

Eight packages, 15,700+ downloads · Paper H submitted

The npm portfolio reaches eight packages and 15,700+ cumulative downloads, six above 1,000 each. Paper H (verbatim fixture recall in LLM-generated test data) → Springer Empirical Software Engineering. First DEV.to launch post for bdd2pw.

Upcoming
